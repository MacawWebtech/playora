Help

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages do you cater for?
We welcome children from 1 to 12 years across age-specific zones.
Do I need to book in advance?
Yes — booking ensures a slot in the right zone. Walk-ins are subject to availability.
Are socks required?
Grip socks are required and available for purchase at reception.
Can parents stay with children?
Yes. Parents are welcome in all zones and can use the lounge with live camera feed.
How do memberships work?
Choose a plan, activate in the parent dashboard, and book sessions with priority access.
