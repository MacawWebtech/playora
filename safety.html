Every Visit

Six Layers of Everyday Safety

Simple routines work together to create a cleaner, calmer and more controlled play environment.

Equipment Checks

Play structures, padding, fasteners and high-use equipment are checked for visible wear or hazards.

Clean Play Areas

High-touch areas are cleaned regularly, with deeper cleaning routines planned around operating hours.

Active Supervision

Team members watch busy zones, encourage safe behavior and step in when children need guidance.

Trained Team

Staff receive practical instruction on play-zone rules, incident response and guest support.

Clear Zone Rules

Entry guidance, age recommendations and activity rules help families know how to use each area safely.

Emergency Access

Walkways and exits are kept clear so staff and families can move efficiently when assistance is needed.

Play Smart Together

Simple Rules Make Play Better for Everyone

Parents, guardians, children and our team all contribute to a safe and enjoyable visit.

For Parents & Guardians

Help us keep every visit smooth.

  • Choose the play zone that matches your child’s age and confidence level.
  • Share important needs or concerns with the team before play begins.
  • Keep personal items outside active play paths and follow posted zone guidance.
  • Encourage breaks for water and rest when children become tired.

For Little Explorers

Four easy habits for happier play.

  • Listen to team members and follow the rules shown in each play zone.
  • Take turns, give other children space and keep hands kind.
  • Use equipment the way it is designed and stay inside your play area.
  • Tell an adult or team member straight away if something feels unsafe.
